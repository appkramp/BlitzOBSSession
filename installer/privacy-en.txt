Privacy — Blitz Session Stats

Until a key is entered in Tools → Blitz Session Stats…, the plugin sends nothing over the network.

Once a key is entered, the plugin connects to the statistics server stats.appkramp.com and sends it:
  • the key;
  • the plugin's version, OBS's version and the operating system;
  • the chosen region and the start of the period;
  • the IDs of the accounts you add as separate sources.
It receives the battle statistics of those accounts.

The vehicle catalogue (tank names, tiers and classes) is downloaded from cdn.appkramp.com; none of your data is sent with it.

As with any connection, the servers see the computer's IP address. The plugin collects and sends no scenes, video, audio, files or other data. A logo picture is only read from your disk.

To stop all connections, remove the key in the plugin's settings, or uninstall the plugin.
